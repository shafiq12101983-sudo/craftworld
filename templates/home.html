Platform Protocols

Identity Verification

To maintain ecosystem integrity, a valid ID and Selfie are required for all withdrawals. This prevents botting and secures your balance.

Payout Efficiency

Withdrawals are processed via Cryptomus. Your rank determines the Vether-to-USD ratio, starting from 15,000 down to 500.

System Spread

A fixed 20% system spread is applied to all liquidations to maintain the Vether liquidity pool and reward long-term holders.
